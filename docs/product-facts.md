## Official controls

- Move: WASD or left joystick
- Interact: E or controller A
- Jump: Space or controller B
- Change outfit: I or controller Y
- Drop carried item: F or controller X
- Open task list: T or D-pad up
- Reveal task names while the list is open: I or controller Y

## Current-build facts

The current playable build contains 11 tracked objectives:

1. Fix the leak
2. Find a different song
3. Collect all bottles
4. Find the telescope
5. Cheer the sad ghost up
6. Find the missing barbell
7. Free a spirit
8. Clean the island
9. Help sell a house
10. Collect all flowers
11. Tell your parents!

Verified collectible totals:

- 10 Ghoul-Aid bottles
- 6 flowers
- 10 purple cleaning puddles

Verified outfit rows:

- Trait: Friendly, Talented, Silly, Strong, Cool
- Movement: Wandering, Quick, Flying, Magnetic
- Occupation: Ghost, Engineer, Musician, Cleaner, Salesperson

Verified dependency facts:

- Find the Telescope unlocks Talented.
- Cheer the Sad Ghost Up requires Silly and unlocks Strong.
- Find the Missing Barbell requires Strong and unlocks Quick.
- Find a Different Song uses Talented and Musician.
- Returning to the trumpet ghost after the song handoff completes Free a Spirit and unlocks Flying.
- Flying works as a second jump and float; it is not unrestricted flight.
- Collect All Flowers has six pickups and unlocks Cleaner.
- Collect All Bottles has ten pickups and unlocks Engineer.
- Fix the Leak requires Strong and Engineer and unlocks Magnetic.
- Clean the Island requires Cleaner, has ten puddles, and unlocks Cool.
- Help Sell a House requires Cool and Salesperson. Flying is useful for reaching the remote buyer.
- Returning to the parents after the other objectives triggers the ending photo.

The site numbers tasks by a recommended dependency route. It does not present that numbering as an official task order.

## Official troubleshooting notes

- Linux and macOS are marked unsupported on the official page.
- Picking up two items at the same time can ruin a run or softlock progress.
- The downloadable Windows build is recommended when browser performance or input is poor.
- A visual quality setting is available.
- The official page mentions Chrome's GameInput data fetcher flag for controller issues.
