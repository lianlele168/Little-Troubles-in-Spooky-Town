Where to start

Speak to the flower-covered ghost outside the green shop near the lighthouse. A gust scattered six flowers around town.

Area: Town and floating docks
Required: Flying for the offshore flower
Reward: Cleaner occupation
Step-by-step solution
1
Start at the flower shop

Talk to the flower ghost so the collection counter and return dialogue are active.

2
Sweep the streets and beaches

Pick up the visible flowers along the central paths, beach edges, and raised town sections.

3
Use Flying over the water

One flower sits on a small green floating platform off the lower beach. Double-jump and float between platforms.

4
Return all six

Go back to the flower ghost after the sixth pickup to finish the task and unlock Cleaner.

Common blockers
The game tracks flowers automatically; you do not carry all six at once.
Use T to check whether the objective is crossed out.
