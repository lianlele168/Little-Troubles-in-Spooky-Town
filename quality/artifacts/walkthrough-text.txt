Little Troubles in Spooky Town Walkthrough

COMPLETE 11-TASK ROUTE

Little Troubles in Spooky Town Walkthrough

This route follows the game's dependency chain: unlock movement and jobs first, then clear the three collection tasks and finish the family ending.

Official game
Task tracker
Recommended completion order
1
1. Find the Telescope

Pick up the loose telescope by the small boat and return it to the scholar ghost beside the statue plaza. Requirement: No special outfit. Reward: Talented trait.

2
2. Cheer the Sad Ghost Up

Change your trait to Silly, then speak to the downcast ghost sitting among the gravestones. Requirement: Silly trait. Reward: Strong trait.

3
3. Find the Missing Barbell

Use Strong to lift the barbell near the beach workout area and hand it to the trainer ghost. Requirement: Strong trait. Reward: Quick ability.

4
4. Find a Different Song

Meet Ghost Malone as a Talented Musician, learn another tune from the trumpet ghost, then bring it back. Requirement: Talented + Musician. Reward: Progresses Free a Spirit.

5
5. Free a Spirit

Return to the trumpet ghost after sharing their song so they can finally leave a role they never wanted. Requirement: Finish the song handoff. Reward: Flying ability.

6
6. Collect All Flowers

Collect all 6 scattered flowers, including the one on a small floating platform, then return to the flower-shop ghost. Requirement: Flying for the offshore flower. Reward: Cleaner occupation.

7
7. Collect All Bottles

Recover all 10 Ghoul-Aid bottles spilled around the island and report back to the delivery ghost by the crashed truck. Requirement: Flying recommended. Reward: Engineer occupation.

8
8. Fix the Leak

Wear Strong and Engineer together, then interact with the valve beside the large purple pipe in the upper town. Requirement: Strong + Engineer. Reward: Magnetic ability.

9
9. Clean the Island

Equip Cleaner and remove all 10 purple puddles across the beaches, roads and the tunnel-side parking area. Requirement: Cleaner occupation. Reward: Cool trait.

10
10. Help Sell a House

Take the for-sale lead, fly to the isolated house island, and pitch the home while dressed as a Cool Salesperson. Requirement: Cool + Salesperson; Flying for travel. Reward: Final story progress.

11
11. Tell Your Parents

Return to the parent ghosts near the starting house after the counter reaches 10/11 and finish the farewell conversation. Requirement: Complete the other 10 tasks. Reward: Ending photo.

Why this order works

The telescope, sad ghost, and barbell form a compact opening loop and provide Talented, Strong, and Quick. The two music objectives then unlock Flying, which removes the main traversal barrier.

Flowers unlock Cleaner; bottles unlock Engineer. Those jobs feed directly into cleaning the island and fixing the leak. Cleaning unlocks Cool, the last trait needed to sell the house.

Finish at 10 of 11

When the first ten entries are crossed out, return to the two parent ghosts near the starting home. Complete their farewell conversation to mark Tell Your Parents and show the ending group photo.

The task list can reveal every objective: open it with T, then press I. This is an in-game option, not a cheat code.
