Where to start

Speak to the worried beach ghost near the lower shore. They count ten dirty puddles across the island.

Area: Whole town
Required: Cleaner occupation
Reward: Cool trait
Step-by-step solution
1
Equip Cleaner

Cleaner unlocks after you return all six flowers to the flower-shop ghost.

2
Clean the lower shore

Remove the obvious dark-purple patches around the beach paths and exercise area.

3
Sweep the upper streets

Check around the flower shop, cemetery, central plaza, roadside and raised paths.

4
Check the tunnel parking bay

A commonly missed puddle sits near the tunnel entrance and parked cars. Return to the beach ghost after all ten are gone.

Common blockers

The quest giver reports your current total if one remains.
