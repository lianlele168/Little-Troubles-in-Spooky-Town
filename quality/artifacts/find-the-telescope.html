Find the Telescope Guide

Pick up the loose telescope by the small boat and return it to the scholar ghost beside the statue plaza.

Where to start

Speak to the scholar ghost near the lower side of the central statue. They want to see farther but have misplaced their telescope.

  • Area: East beach to statue plaza
  • Required: No special outfit
  • Reward: Talented trait

Step-by-step solution

  1. 1

    Walk to the far beach

    Follow the lower shoreline toward the small rowboat and short green dock at the edge of town.

  2. 2

    Pick up the telescope

    Interact with the long telescope lying at the beach edge. Your character carries it upright, which looks odd but is correct.

  3. 3

    Return to the statue plaza

    Carry it back to the green-clothed scholar ghost beside the central statue and finish the dialogue.

  4. 4

    Equip Talented when needed

    Completing the handoff unlocks the Talented trait used by the music quest.

Common blockers

  • You can do this immediately; Flying is not required.
  • Do not pick up a second object while carrying the telescope.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for Find the Telescope?

No special outfit. Start in East beach to statue plaza.

What does Find the Telescope unlock?

It rewards Talented trait.
