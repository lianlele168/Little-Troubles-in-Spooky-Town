Where to start

This objective is linked to Find a Different Song and cannot be finished before that tune reaches Ghost Malone.

Area: Flower shop lane
Required: Finish the song handoff
Reward: Flying ability
Step-by-step solution
1
Complete the song request

Deliver the trumpet ghost's tune to Ghost Malone first.

2
Return uphill

Go back to the trumpet ghost beside the flower shop near the lighthouse.

3
Finish the second conversation

Listen as the ghost admits they never liked playing the trumpet. Completing the dialogue frees their spirit.

4
Equip Flying

The reward appears in the middle ability row and opens the offshore routes.

Common blockers
If this does not complete, talk to Ghost Malone again and exhaust the dialogue.
Flying works by pressing Jump a second time in the air.
