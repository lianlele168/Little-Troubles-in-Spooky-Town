Step-by-step solution
1
Activate the bottle request

Finish the delivery ghost's dialogue beside the truck near the cemetery road.

2
Search each elevation

Check the lower beaches, central plaza, rooftops and upper paths. Bottles glow when you are close.

3
Cross to the offshore spot

Use Flying for the bottle placed beyond the normal shoreline route.

4
Return at 10 of 10

Speak to the delivery ghost again. Saving the picnic unlocks the Engineer occupation.
