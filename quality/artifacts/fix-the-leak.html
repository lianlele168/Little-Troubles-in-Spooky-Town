Fix the Leak Guide

Wear Strong and Engineer together, then interact with the valve beside the large purple pipe in the upper town.

Official game Task tracker

Where to start

The hard-hat worker stands beside the purple pipe and valve above the central streets. The dialogue gives both strength and certification hints.

  • Area: Upper pipeworks
  • Required: Strong + Engineer
  • Reward: Magnetic ability

Step-by-step solution

  1. 1

    Unlock both outfit parts

    Strong comes from cheering the sad ghost; Engineer comes from returning all 10 bottles.

  2. 2

    Set the required combination

    Choose Strong on the first row and Engineer on the occupation row. The movement row can be anything.

  3. 3

    Use the pipe valve

    Approach the large purple pipe beside the hard-hat worker and interact with the highlighted valve.

  4. 4

    Collect the Magnetic reward

    The middle-row Magnetic ability lets you grab nearby objects from farther away.

Common blockers

  • Engineer without Strong will not satisfy the valve's strength requirement.
  • The pipe is above the cemetery-side streets, not on the lower beach.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for Fix the Leak?

Strong + Engineer. Start in Upper pipeworks.

What does Fix the Leak unlock?

It rewards Magnetic ability.
