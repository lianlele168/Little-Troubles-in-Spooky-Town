Outfits and Abilities Guide

Outfits are the game's puzzle system. A look combines one trait, one movement ability, and one occupation; NPC hints name the exact pieces they expect.

The three outfit rows

  • Traits: Friendly, Talented, Silly, Strong, and Cool.
  • Movement abilities: Wandering, Quick, Flying, and Magnetic.
  • Occupations: Ghost, Engineer, Musician, Cleaner, and Salesperson.

Task unlocks

  2. 2

    Strong

  7. 7

    Fix the leak while dressed as Strong + Engineer.

  8. 8

    Cool

    Clean all 10 purple puddles around the island.

Important combinations

  • Talented + Musician opens the different-song conversation.
  • Strong + Engineer satisfies both hints at the leaking valve.
  • Cool + Salesperson convinces the remote buyer to take the house.
  • Flying is a travel ability; land first, then switch outfits for the destination conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Are outfits only cosmetic?

No. Strong lifts heavy objects, Flying enables a second jump and float, Magnetic extends pickup reach, and occupations unlock task interactions.

How do I change outfits?

Press I on keyboard or Y on a controller, then move through the three rows and choose one option per row.
