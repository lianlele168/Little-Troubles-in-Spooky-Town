Where to start

The sad ghost is in the cemetery above the central streets. Their hint explicitly asks for someone silly.

Area: Cemetery
Required: Silly trait
Reward: Strong trait
Step-by-step solution
1
Open the outfit menu

Press I on keyboard or Y on a gamepad.

2
Select Silly

Change the top outfit row to Silly. The other two rows do not matter for this conversation.

3
Talk in the cemetery

Approach the sad ghost between the gravestones and complete the short conversation.

4
Keep the Strong unlock

The reward lets you lift the missing barbell and helps with the leaking valve later.

Common blockers
The task will not complete with Friendly or Talented selected.
The cemetery is close to the crashed delivery truck.
