How to reveal the list in game

  1. 1

    Open Tasks

    Press T on keyboard or D-pad up on a controller.

  2. 2

    Reveal hidden names

    Press I or the controller Y button while the task panel is open.

  3. 3

    Check the counter

    The top-right counter moves from 0/11 to 11/11 as full objectives complete.

Frequently asked questions

Why does an official screenshot show 0/10?

Some promotional screenshots show an earlier ten-task counter. The current playable build includes Tell Your Parents and displays 11 total.

Do collectible pickups fill inventory slots?

Flowers and bottles update their collection counters automatically. Hand-carried quest objects such as the telescope and barbell use the carry/drop controls.
