All 11 Little Troubles in Spooky Town Tasks

The current release tracks 11 objectives. Each entry below has a dedicated route with the NPC, outfit combination, handoff, and reward.

Official game
01
Find the Telescope

Pick up the loose telescope by the small boat and return it to the scholar ghost beside the statue plaza.

East beach to statue plaza
No special outfit
Talented trait
02
Cheer the Sad Ghost Up

Change your trait to Silly, then speak to the downcast ghost sitting among the gravestones.

Cemetery
Silly trait
Strong trait
03
Find the Missing Barbell

Use Strong to lift the barbell near the beach workout area and hand it to the trainer ghost.

Beach gym
Strong trait
Quick ability
04
Find a Different Song

Meet Ghost Malone as a Talented Musician, learn another tune from the trumpet ghost, then bring it back.

Central street and flower shop
Talented + Musician
Progresses Free a Spirit
05
Free a Spirit

Return to the trumpet ghost after sharing their song so they can finally leave a role they never wanted.

Flower shop lane
Finish the song handoff
Flying ability
06
Collect All Flowers

Collect all 6 scattered flowers, including the one on a small floating platform, then return to the flower-shop ghost.

Town and floating docks
Flying for the offshore flower
Cleaner occupation
07
Collect All Bottles

Recover all 10 Ghoul-Aid bottles spilled around the island and report back to the delivery ghost by the crashed truck.

Whole island
Flying recommended
Engineer occupation
08
Fix the Leak

Wear Strong and Engineer together, then interact with the valve beside the large purple pipe in the upper town.

Upper pipeworks
Strong + Engineer
Magnetic ability
09
Clean the Island

Equip Cleaner and remove all 10 purple puddles across the beaches, roads and the tunnel-side parking area.

Whole town
Cleaner occupation
Cool trait
10
Help Sell a House

Take the for-sale lead, fly to the isolated house island, and pitch the home while dressed as a Cool Salesperson.

For-sale house to offshore island
Cool + Salesperson; Flying for travel
Final story progress
11
Tell Your Parents

Return to the parent ghosts near the starting house after the counter reaches 10/11 and finish the farewell conversation.

Starting home
Complete the other 10 tasks
Ending photo
How to reveal the list in game
1
Open Tasks

Press T on keyboard or D-pad up on a controller.

2
Reveal hidden names

Press I or the controller Y button while the task panel is open.

3
Check the counter

The top-right counter moves from 0/11 to 11/11 as full objectives complete.
