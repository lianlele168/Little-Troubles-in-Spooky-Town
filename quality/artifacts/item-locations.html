Item and Collectible Locations

There are two tracked collectible sets plus several hand-carried quest objects. Use a clockwise sweep and finish carried-item handoffs before touching another object.

Official game Task tracker

Totals

  • 6 flowers for the flower-shop ghost.
  • 10 Ghoul-Aid bottles for the delivery ghost.
  • 10 purple puddles for the Cleaner objective.
  • One carried telescope and one carried barbell.

Efficient collection sweep

  1. 1

    Lower beach

    Check the gym, rowboat, docks, shoreline corners, and the path up to the statue.

  2. 2

    Central plaza

    Circle the statue, musician street, parked cars, and lower roofs.

  3. 3

    Upper town

    Search the cemetery, crashed truck, pipeworks, flower shop, and lighthouse paths.

  4. 4

    Offshore platforms

    Equip Flying and clear the small green platforms and remote island last.

Avoid the carry softlock

The developer specifically warns that picking up two items at once can ruin the run. Telescope and barbell are hand-carried objects; deliver the one you have or press F to drop it before interacting with another carried item.

Flowers and bottles are counter pickups and do not need to be carried back one by one.

Frequently asked questions

How many bottles are there?

There are 10 bottle pickups in the current build.

How many flowers are there?

There are 6 flowers. One sits on a floating platform and is easiest after Flying unlocks.

Where is the commonly missed puddle?

Check the tunnel-side parking bay and cars after sweeping the obvious beach and street puddles.
