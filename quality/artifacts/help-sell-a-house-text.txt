Where to start

Talk to the real-estate ghost beside the marked house. They need someone cool enough to convince a buyer.

Area: For-sale house to offshore island
Required: Cool + Salesperson; Flying for travel
Reward: Final story progress
Step-by-step solution
1
Speak to the real-estate ghost

Start the request at the house with the sale sign on the right side of town.

2
Equip Flying for the crossing

Use the offshore green platforms to reach the isolated island with another ghost.

3
Switch to the sales outfit

Once safely on the island, select Cool on the trait row and Salesperson on the occupation row.

4
Pitch the house

Talk to the prospective buyer and finish the basement dialogue. The task completes during this conversation.

Common blockers
Cool unlocks after cleaning all 10 puddles.
You can land first with Flying, then change the middle ability without falling.
