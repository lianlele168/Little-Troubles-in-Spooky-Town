Find a Different Song Guide

Meet Ghost Malone as a Talented Musician, learn another tune from the trumpet ghost, then bring it back.

Where to start

The pan-flute performer stands on the central street with music notes floating nearby. They will only work with a talented musician.

  • Area: Central street and flower shop
  • Required: Talented + Musician
  • Reward: Progresses Free a Spirit

Step-by-step solution

  1. 1

    Dress for the conversation

    Select Talented on the trait row and Musician on the occupation row.

  2. 2

    Talk to Ghost Malone

    Speak to the pan-flute ghost in the central street and accept the request for a different song.

  3. 3

    Visit the trumpet ghost

    Go uphill toward the flower-fronted shop near the lighthouse and talk to the other musician.

  4. 4

    Return with the tune

    Go back to Ghost Malone and complete the dialogue. Then revisit the trumpet ghost for the linked spirit task.

Common blockers

  • Talented comes from the telescope task.
  • Music notes above a character make both musicians easy to spot.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for Find a Different Song?

Talented + Musician. Start in Central street and flower shop.

What does Find a Different Song unlock?

It rewards Progresses Free a Spirit.
